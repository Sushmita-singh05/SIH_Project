Grounded in your circuit operations and simulation shots

Your circuit applies a Hadamard (H) gate to q0.

The H gate transforms the initial |0⟩ state into an equal superposition of |0⟩ and |1⟩.

That is why the measurement results are approximately 50% |0⟩ and 50% |1⟩.

In the Bloch sphere, this corresponds to the |+⟩ state on the equator.

✓ Mathematical verification: Applying H|0⟩ yields (|0⟩ + |1⟩) / √2. Squaring amplitude (1/√2)² = 1/2 confirms 50% statistical distribution.
Why this matters

Understanding the relationship between your circuit, measurement results, and quantum concepts helps you build stronger intuition instead of memorizing outcomes.
